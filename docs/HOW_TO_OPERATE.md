## Getting started

This chapter takes you from your first sign-in to a fully set-up company, then shows how to find your way around and which routines keep the books, taxes and filings up to date. Follow the first-week setup in the order given: later steps (invoices, bills, payroll, returns) read the details you enter in the earlier ones. The daily, weekly, month-end and year-end checklists at the end are the rhythm of a well-run office.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Holds every permission and cannot be locked out. Runs the setup wizard, changes company details, sets approval rules and role permissions, and can invite people with any role, including Admin. |
| Managing Partner | Holds every permission, like Admin. Can invite people with any role except Admin. |
| Partner | Holds every permission, like Admin. Can invite people with any role except Admin. |
| Finance Manager | By default: sales (including issuing invoices), accounting, purchases, GST and TDS, payroll figures, compliance filings, bank accounts and issuing documents; views partners, projects, approvals, reports and the employee directory. Cannot change company details or invite people. |
| HR Manager | By default: employee records, attendance, leave and payroll, Workplace (meetings, tasks, announcements), issuing documents and viewing reports and approvals. Can invite people as Employee or Project Manager only. |
| Project Manager | By default: creates and changes projects, budgets and milestones, manages Workplace items, issues documents, and views sales, purchases, CRM, the employee directory, approvals and reports. |
| Employee | By default: views company details and Workplace, issues documents (letters, quotations, minutes) and uses the self-service pages such as My Profile, My Leave and My Payslips. |

**Before you start**

- You need a username and password. The first administrator gets them from whoever installed the system; everyone else joins through an invite link (see the task Join with an invite link).
- Keep the LLP's papers to hand for setup: incorporation certificate (LLPIN), PAN, TAN, GST registration certificate, registered office address, bank account details and the company logo as a PNG or JPEG file.
- Decide the date you start keeping books in this system (for example 1 April 2026) and get the closing balances from your previous books as at the day before (31 March 2026).
- Defaults for the four non-partner roles can be changed by an Admin under Administration > Users and Access > Role permissions. This chapter describes the defaults.

### Sign in

- **Who:** Everyone
- **Where:** Sign in page (`/login`)

1. Open the system address in your browser. If you are not signed in you see the Sign in page with the name Tera2Nano ERP.
2. Type your Username and Password.
3. Click Sign in.
4. If you see "Enter your username and password.", one of the boxes was empty. If you see "Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again.", wait one minute before trying again.

**What happens next:** You land on the module launcher (Choose a module), or on the page you were trying to open before signing in. If the company has not been set up yet, an administrator is taken straight to the setup wizard; anyone else sees "The company has not been set up yet. Ask a company administrator to complete the setup."

**Tips**

- To sign out, click your name at the top right and choose Sign out, or use Sign out on the launcher. The next person to sign in then starts at the launcher.
- Every sign-in, failed attempt and sign-out is recorded in Administration > Users and Access > Login history.

### Join with an invite link

- **Who:** New members
- **Where:** Invite link received from the company (`/join/<token>`)

1. Open the link you were given. The page checks the link (Checking your link…).
2. When you see Join followed by the company name, fill in the questions shown, including your full name, a username (no spaces) and your password typed twice.
3. Click Create my account.
4. When you see You are all set, click Go to sign in and sign in with your new username and password.

**What happens next:** Your account is created with the access role the person who invited you chose. The link works once only; afterwards it shows This link cannot be used.

**Tips**

- If the link has expired or was revoked, ask for a new one. You never choose your own role.

### Run the first-time company setup wizard

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Company setup wizard (opens automatically; later from your name menu > Resume company setup) (`/setup`)

1. Step 1 Company name: enter the legal name (for example Tera2Nano Technologies LLP) and the display name (for example Tera2Nano). Click Create company and continue.
2. Step 2 Incorporation and registration: fill in what you have (PAN, LLPIN, date of incorporation and so on). Leave anything you do not have blank.
3. Step 3 Registered office address: add the registered office.
4. Step 4 Company contacts: add the general, sales, finance or HR contacts that print on documents.
5. Step 5 Tax registrations: record only registrations that really apply. Do not enter a GSTIN unless the company is registered.
6. Step 6 Bank details: add the bank account, or skip if it is not opened yet.
7. Step 7 Company logo: upload a PNG or JPEG.
8. Step 8 Letterhead and footer: design the letterhead used on every PDF.
9. Step 9 Numbering sequences: check the prefixes used for document numbers.
10. Step 10 Financial year: add the current year, normally 1 April to 31 March.
11. Step 11 Default payment terms: create a draft of your payment terms and activate it when ready.
12. Move between steps with Back and Continue. On optional steps you can click Skip for now. Click any step in the left list to jump to it.
13. Step 12 Review and save: check the summary and click Save company profile, then Go to Company Overview.

**What happens next:** The company is created at step 1. Progress is saved as you go, so you can click Exit and finish later and come back through your name menu > Resume company setup. Skipped steps show Skipped – add later and do not block setup. After saving, a list Still to do (not blocking) shows anything left.

**Tips**

- Only a company administrator (someone who may change company details) can run the wizard; others see "Only a company administrator can run company setup."
- Everything entered in the wizard can be changed later in the Company module.

- **Where:** Company > Document Numbering (`/company/numbering`)

1. Open Company > Document Numbering.
2. Check the prefixes. Numbers carry your short company code, the document code, the two-digit year and the day and month, for example T2N/INV/26/2411/00001 for an invoice or T2N-LD-26-2411-0001 for a CRM lead.

**What happens next:** Numbers are allocated automatically when a document is issued; you never type them. A number is never reused, even when a document is voided.

**Tips**

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Company > Financial Year Settings (`/company/financial-years`)

1. Open Company > Financial Year Settings.
2. Check that the current year exists, for example 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Add it if it is missing.
3. Save.

**What happens next:** Numbering, reports, budgets and the compliance calendar use the financial year.

**Tips**

- Add next year's financial year before 1 April so numbering continues smoothly.

### Week 1, step 9: review the chart of accounts

- **Who:** Finance Manager, Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Accounting > Chart of Accounts (`/accounting/accounts`)

1. Open the Accounting tile and click Chart of Accounts.
2. Read through the accounts grouped by type.
3. To add one you need (for example a second bank or a specific expense head), click New account, fill in the details and click Save.

**What happens next:** Invoices, bills, payments and payroll post automatically to these accounts.

**Tips**

### Week 1, step 10: enter opening balances

- **Who:** Finance Manager, Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Accounting > Opening balances (`/accounting/opening-balances`)

1. Open Accounting > Opening balances.
2. Set Balances as at, usually the day before your first transaction (for example 31 March 2026).
3. Use Find an account to locate each account and type its Debit or Credit (for example Bank: Debit ₹4,50,000; Partners' capital: Credit ₹10,00,000).
4. Watch Total debit, Total credit and Difference at the bottom.
5. Click Post opening balances.

**What happens next:** The balances are posted to the books. If debits and credits do not match, the difference goes to Opening balance adjustments so the books still balance; check the figures first if you did not expect a difference.

**Tips**

- Vendor opening balances can also be entered on each vendor (Opening balance we owe).

### Week 1, step 11: set approval rules

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Approvals > Approval Rules (`/approvals/rules`)

1. Open the Approvals tile and click Approval Rules.
2. Click New rule.
3. Enter a Rule name (for example Bills above ₹50,000) and choose Applies to: Purchase order, Vendor bill, Leave request, Expense, Payroll run, Project budget, Salary revision, Payment, Partner withdrawal or Quotation.
4. Set Amount from and Amount to (both included; leave empty for no limit).
5. Under Approval steps, in order, choose who approves: Anyone with a role or A specific user, and whether it Needs Any one of them or All of them. Optionally set Escalate after (days). Use Add step for a second approver.
6. Keep Rule is switched on ticked and click Add rule.

**What happens next:** From now on, a record of that kind within the amount range waits for approval before it is numbered or posted. Approvers see it under Approvals > Pending My Approval and in the approvals bell.

**Tips**

- Without any rule, nothing is held for approval.

- **Who:** Finance Manager, Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Compliance > Compliance Dashboard (`/compliance`)

1. Open the Compliance tile.
2. On the Compliance Dashboard click Generate calendar (or, on Compliance > Filings, Generate calendar for the year shown).
3. Open Compliance > Calendar to see the due dates.

**What happens next:** Due dates for the financial year are created from the obligations catalogue. Later, the same button reads Refresh calendar.

### Week 1, step 13: check the tax settings

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** Company > Tax Configuration > GST settings tab (`/company/tax`)

1. Open Company > Tax Configuration.
2. On the GST settings tab tick GST applies to this company only if the company is registered, then choose the GST treatment, Default tax code (for example 18%) and Default place of supply, and type the Invoice title (for example Tax Invoice).
3. Choose what prints: Show GSTIN on documents, Show HSN/SAC codes, Show place of supply, Show CGST/SGST/IGST breakup.
4. Save. Use the Calculator tab to check a sample: Taxable value (INR) ₹1,00,000 at GST rate (%) 18 within the same state gives CGST ₹9,000 and SGST ₹9,000.

**What happens next:** Quotations, invoices and bills work out GST from these settings. While GST is off, documents show "GST is not switched on for the company (Tax Configuration)" and print without tax.

### Week 1, step 14: set up email

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner (with your IT person for the mail server)
- **Where:** Administration > Email settings (`/company/email`)

1. Ask your IT person to enter the company mail server in the server settings (EMAIL_HOST, EMAIL_PORT, EMAIL_HOST_USER, EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD and DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL, see the README) and restart the app.

3. Fill Sender name, Reply-to address and a Signature, and click Save settings.

5. Optionally tick "Email payment reminders automatically".

**What happens next:** Invoices, credit notes, quotations, purchase orders, payslips, payment reminders and invitation links can now be emailed from their pages, with the PDF attached. Every message is listed under Administration > Sent emails.

**Tips**

### Daily routine

- **Who:** Finance Manager, partners, anyone who handles money
- **Where:** Approvals, Sales, Purchases, CRM (`/approvals`)

1. Open the approvals bell and decide everything waiting for you.
2. Sales > Payments Received: record every payment that reached the bank today and apply it to invoices.
3. Sales > Invoices and Notes: issue the drafts that are ready.
4. Purchases > Bills: enter vendor bills received today and attach the scanned bill.
5. CRM > Activities: work through Today and Overdue.

**What happens next:** Receivables, payables and the bank stay current, so dashboards and reminders are accurate.

### Weekly routine

- **Who:** Finance Manager, partners
- **Where:** Sales, Purchases, Compliance, CRM (`/sales/reminders`)

1. Sales > Reminders: work through Due now (Send or Skip each).
2. Sales > Receivables Ageing: follow up anything in the older columns.
3. Purchases > Payables Ageing: check Due in the next 30 days and plan vendor payments.
4. Compliance > Calendar: look at what falls due in the coming weeks.
5. CRM > Deals: review deals past their expected close date.

**What happens next:** Nothing important slips past its due date.

### Month-end close checklist

- **Who:** Finance Manager, HR Manager (payroll), partners
- **Where:** Several modules

1. Bank reconciliation: Accounting > Import statement for each bank account, then Bank reconciliation > Match lines (accept suggestions, create missing entries, ignore what should not be matched) and Reconciliation > Mark reconciled up to the statement date. Cash accounts are ticked by hand in Bank and Cash > Reconcile.
2. Recurring invoices: Sales > Recurring invoices. If Create N due now appears, click it and confirm Create now; issue any drafts it made.
3. Payment reminders: Sales > Reminders > Due now. Send or Skip each reminder; Send emails it to the customer when email is set up (or switch on "Email payment reminders automatically" under Rules and wording).
4. Vendor bills: Purchases > Bills. Record every bill for the month, check the Draft and Pending approval chips are cleared.
5. Payroll run and posting: People (HR) > Payroll > New payroll run. Check the payslips, then Lock run, Send for approval, Post to books and, once salaries are paid, Mark salaries paid.
6. Depreciation run: Accounting > Fixed Assets > Depreciation run tab. Check the preview and click Post depreciation.
7. GST returns: GST & Tax > Returns. Create the month's return record (New return record), prepare the figures and, after filing on the GST portal, click Mark as filed.
8. TDS: GST & Tax > TDS. Record challan for TDS deposited (Challan 281) and, in the quarter-end month, add the TDS return with New TDS return.
9. Compliance filings: Compliance > Filings. Open each task due this month and click Mark filed after filing.
10. Reports review: Accounting > Financial Reports (profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, cash flow) and Reports > Executive Dashboard.

**What happens next:** The month's books are complete and reconciled, tax liabilities are recorded and filings are marked, so the next month starts clean.

**Tips**

- Do bank reconciliation first: it often reveals unrecorded receipts and payments.
- Payroll posting records the salary cost and the amounts payable to employees, PF, ESI, professional tax and tax authorities.

### Year-end checklist

- **Who:** Partners, Finance Manager
- **Where:** Partners, Compliance, Accounting, Company

1. Finish the March month-end close checklist first.
2. Profit sharing: Partners > Profit Sharing > Allocate profit. Preview the year's profit, click Save as draft, check the shares and click Post to the books.
3. Partner statements: Partners > Statements. Choose each partner and period, click Show, then Download PDF or Export CSV.
4. Annual filings: Compliance > Filings. Check the annual LLP and income-tax filings on the calendar and click Mark filed as each is done.
5. Next year's budget: Accounting > Budgets > New budget, fill in the figures, click Create budget and then Make active.
6. Company > Financial Year Settings: make sure next year exists.
7. Compliance Dashboard: Generate calendar for the new financial year.

**What happens next:** Profit is allocated to partners' accounts in the books, statements are ready for the partners, annual filings are tracked and the new year is ready to use.

**Tips**

- A posted profit allocation can be undone only with Reverse, which keeps a record.

- Clear the approvals bell.
- Record payments received and apply them to invoices.
- Issue ready draft invoices.
- Enter vendor bills received and attach the scans.

**Every week**

- Allocate and post partners' profit share.
- Download partner statements.
- Track and mark annual filings.
- Create and activate next year's budget.

## CRM

CRM is where you track people and companies that might buy from you, from the first enquiry to a won order. A lead is converted into a contact, an account (the company) and usually a deal; deals move through pipeline stages on a board until they are won or lost. Winning a deal links or creates the sales customer, and Create quotation opens a quotation already filled in from the deal.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Everything in CRM: leads, import, conversion, contacts, accounts, deals, activities, products and CRM Settings (permissions crm.view and crm.manage). |
| Project Manager | Views leads, contacts, accounts, deals, activities and CRM Reports (crm.view) and can export CSV files; cannot create or change anything. |
| Finance Manager, HR Manager, Employee | No CRM access by default. An Admin can grant crm.view or crm.manage under Administration > Users and Access > Role permissions. |

**Before you start**

- Ask an Admin to check CRM > CRM Settings: the pipeline stages (Qualification 10%, Needs Analysis 25%, Proposal/Quotation 50%, Negotiation 75%, Closed Won, Closed Lost), the lead sources and the lost reasons are ready-made and can be changed.
- Optionally add your products and services with their prices and GST rates under CRM Settings > Products, so deal line items can be picked from a list.
- Lead and deal numbers are given automatically, for example T2N-LD-26-2411-0001 for a lead and T2N-DL-26-2411-0001 for a deal.
- Every lead, contact, account and deal can have an Owner. Owners are chosen from the company's users.

### Read the CRM Dashboard

- **Who:** Anyone with CRM access
- **Where:** CRM > CRM Dashboard (`/crm`)

1. Open the CRM tile on the launcher; the CRM Dashboard opens.
2. Read the tiles: Open pipeline, Weighted forecast (amount x stage probability), Win rate, Won in 12 months, Open leads and My activities. Click a tile to open the matching list.
3. Look at the Sales funnel, Pipeline by stage, Lead sources, Won and lost deals, Top owners and Forecast by month.
4. Use New lead, New deal or New activity at the top to start work.

**What happens next:** Nothing is changed. The figures update as leads, deals and activities change.

**Tips**

- My activities turns red when you have overdue activities.

### Add a lead

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner, Partner
- **Where:** CRM > Leads > New lead (`/crm/leads?new=1`)

## Sales

Sales is where you bill customers and collect money: customers, quotations, invoices, proforma invoices, credit and debit notes, payments received, recurring invoices and payment reminders. GST (CGST and SGST, or IGST) is worked out from the customer's place of supply, every issued document gets an automatic number and a PDF on your letterhead, and invoices, notes and payments post to the books by themselves.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Everything in Sales, including voiding issued quotations and deciding approvals. |
| Finance Manager | Adds and edits customers, prepares and issues invoices and notes, records and voids payments, runs recurring invoices and reminders, and creates invoices from quotations (sales.view, sales.manage, sales.issue). |
| Project Manager | Views customers, invoices, payments and reports (sales.view) and can issue quotations; cannot create invoices or record payments. |
| Employee, HR Manager | Can open Sales > Quotations and issue quotations (documents.issue). The other Sales pages need sales.view. |

**Before you start**

- Company > Tax Configuration: GST must be switched on (GST applies to this company) for tax to be charged. While it is off, documents say "GST is not switched on for the company (Tax Configuration)" and print without tax.
- Company > Policies and Default Terms: active payment terms (for example 30 days) and default terms print on quotations and invoices and set the default due date.
- Company > Bank Accounts: add the account so bank details can print for payment and payments can be marked Deposited to it.
- Approvals > Approval Rules: a rule for Quotation holds large quotations for approval; a rule for Payment holds voiding a large payment.
- Sales > Customers: give each customer an email address so invoices, quotations and reminders can be emailed to them. Email itself is set up under Administration > Email settings.
- Numbers are automatic: for example T2N/QUO/26/2411/00001 (quotation), T2N/INV/26/2411/00001 (invoice), T2N/PI/… (proforma), T2N/CN/… (credit note), T2N/DN/… (debit note), T2N/PAY/… (payment), T2N-CUS-26-2411-0001 (customer) and T2N-RIN-26-2411-0001 (recurring profile).

### Issue a quotation

- **Who:** Anyone who may issue documents (all roles by default)
- **Where:** Sales > Quotations (`/company/quotations`)

1. Open Sales > Quotations (or + New > Quotation). The notice Quotation ID shows the number it will get.
2. Under Customer enter the Customer name, the Address (one line per row), Customer GSTIN (optional) and Customer state (place of supply), or leave the state on automatic.
3. Set the Quotation date and Valid until (leave empty for the company's validity period), and optionally Customer's own reference, Subject and Introduction.
4. Under Items enter each item: description, HSN/SAC (for example 998314), Quantity, Unit, Rate (INR), Discount % and GST %. Click Add item for more. The totals with GST appear as you type.
5. Optionally fill in Key details (for example Completion period: 12 weeks) and Scope and conditions sections, Notes and Terms and conditions (empty prints the company default terms).
6. Tick Print the bank details for payment and the Authorised Signatory block if wanted.
7. Click Issue quotation and confirm Issue quotation.

**What happens next:** If no approval rule applies, the quotation is issued at once with its number (for example T2N/QUO/26/2411/00001) and PDFs, and appears under Issued quotations. If it is above an approval limit, it is Sent for approval instead: it has no number or PDF yet and waits under Waiting for approval and drafts.

**Tips**

- An issued quotation cannot be edited. If it is wrong, void it and issue a new one.
- A quotation started from a CRM deal is already filled in and is linked back to the deal.

5. Click Delete to remove a draft you no longer need.

**What happens next:** When approved, the quotation is issued exactly as if it had been issued directly: it gets its number and PDF. A rejected quotation comes back as a draft with the reason.

**Tips**

- Cancel the correction leaves the edit without sending.

### Sales: connections to other modules

## Purchases

Purchases covers everything you buy: vendors, internal purchase requests, vendor quotations and their comparison, purchase orders with approval, receiving goods, vendor bills with input tax credit (ITC) and TDS, payments to vendors applied to bills, and the payables ageing. Recording a bill or a payment posts it to the books automatically, and large orders, bills and payments can be held for approval.

## Accounting

Accounting holds the company's books: the chart of accounts, every journal entry, cash and bank balances, fixed assets and budgets. Invoices, payments, vendor bills, payroll and partner transactions post here by themselves; in this module you record the money that moves outside those documents (expenses and other income), make manual journal entries, import bank statements and match them to the books, reconcile the bank and read the financial statements. Every report (trial balance, ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow) is read straight from the posted entries.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing partner, Partner | Hold every permission: record, reverse, reconcile, run depreciation, manage budgets and read every report. |
| Finance manager | Day-to-day bookkeeper by default (accounting.view and accounting.manage): records expenses and income, posts journal entries, reconciles bank accounts, enters opening balances, manages fixed assets and budgets. |
| HR manager, Project manager, Employee | No access to the Accounting module by default. An admin can grant accounting.view (read only) or accounting.manage in Administration > Users and Access. |

**Before you start**

- Open the Accounting tile from the module launcher. The left menu shows: Accounting Dashboard, Expenses and Income, Bank and Cash, Bank reconciliation, Import statement, Journal, Fixed Assets, Budgets, Chart of Accounts, Opening balances, Financial Reports.
- Pages that change the books (Record expense, New journal entry, Reconcile, Transfer, Import statement, Accept, Create entry and similar buttons) appear only for people holding accounting.manage. Opening balances needs accounting.manage even to open.
- Make sure the company's financial years are set in Company > Financial Year Settings. Nothing can be posted with a date inside a locked financial year.
- Expense and income numbers, journal references and fixed asset codes come from Company > Document Numbering.
- GST can only be entered on an expense or income when GST is switched on for the company (Company > Tax Configuration).
- The first time, use Chart of Accounts > Set up the books so all standard accounts exist.

### Import a bank statement

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner
- **Where:** Accounting > Import statement (`/accounting/bank-import`)

1. Download the statement from your bank's internet banking as CSV, Excel (.xlsx or .xls), a text-based PDF, or OFX. A scanned or photographed PDF cannot be read; download the statement as CSV or Excel instead.
2. Open Accounting > Import statement (or Bank reconciliation > Import on the account).
3. Choose the Bank account and the Statement file, then click Upload and read.
4. Check the columns. Row with the column names is found below the account details at the top of the file; Date, Description / narration, Reference / cheque no., Withdrawal (money out), Deposit (money in) and Balance are guessed from the headings. If the statement has one amount column, choose it under Single amount column and pick the Dr / Cr column if there is one.
5. Leave Dates are written on Day first for Indian statements (05/08/2026 is 5 August).
6. Click Check. The summary shows rows read, how many will be imported, how many were already imported, heading and total rows skipped, and each row with a problem and why. The first lines are listed with Money in, Money out and Balance.
7. If the figures look right, click Import N lines.

**What happens next:** The statement's lines are added to the bank account, ready to match. Import finished shows the summary and a Match the lines button. The file is kept privately and listed under Statements imported for this account, where it can be downloaded again.

**Tips**

- Importing a statement that overlaps an earlier one is safe: lines already imported are skipped and counted as Already imported.
- The column choices are remembered for the account, so the next statement from the same bank is set up for you.
- A wrong import can be taken out with Remove while none of its lines is matched or ignored.
- Lines dated on or before the date the account was last reconciled are not imported; undo that reconciliation first if a line is really missing.
- Live bank feeds are not connected; statements always come from a file.

### Match statement lines to the books

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner
- **Where:** Accounting > Bank reconciliation > Match lines (`/accounting/bank-reconciliation`)

1. Open Accounting > Bank reconciliation and click Match lines on the account. The To match chip lists the lines not matched yet.
2. Under each line are the suggested entries from the books with Sure match, Likely match or Possible match and the reasons (same amount, how many days apart, a reference or name found in the bank's narration).
3. Click Accept on the right suggestion. To accept every sure match at once, click Accept all sure matches.
4. If the suggestion is wrong or missing, click Find entry, search by reference, name or amount and tick the entry. Tick several entries when one bank line covers them (for example one deposit for two customer payments); Match is allowed when the Difference is zero.
5. When several statement lines make up one entry, tick the lines' boxes and click Match N selected lines together.
6. For a line that should not be matched (for example a bank error reversed the next day), click Ignore and give the reason.

**What happens next:** Matched lines move to the Matched chip, showing the entry they were matched to; ignored lines move to Ignored with the reason. Each match, undo and ignore is written to the company change history.

**Tips**

- Undo match on the Matched chip puts the line back to match. It is not offered once the account is reconciled past that date.
- Stop ignoring on the Ignored chip brings an ignored line back.
- Ignored lines still appear in the reconciliation report as money on the statement that is not in the books.

### Create the missing entry from a statement line

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner (customer payments also need sales.manage, vendor payments purchases.manage)
- **Where:** Accounting > Bank reconciliation > Match lines > Create entry (`/accounting/bank-reconciliation`)

6. Transfer: choose the other cash or bank account.
7. Click Record and match.

**What happens next:** The payment, expense, income or transfer is recorded on the line's date for its amount, posted to the books with its own number, and matched to the line straight away.

**Tips**

- The usual rules apply: a vendor payment or expense above an approval rule waits for approval. The line then shows that an entry is waiting; once it is approved, it is offered as a Sure match on the line.
- Undoing the match later leaves the entry in the books. Void it on its own page if it was wrong.
- Customer and vendor payments can be created from a statement of any bank account, whether or not it is linked to a Company > Bank Accounts record. For one that is not linked, the payment posts straight to that account's ledger; for one that is, it shows the bank's name as usual.

### Reconcile the bank account for the month

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner (anyone with accounting.view can read the report)
- **Where:** Accounting > Bank reconciliation > Reconciliation (`/accounting/bank-reconciliation`)

1. Import the month's statement and match or ignore every line (see the tasks above).
2. Open Accounting > Bank reconciliation and click Reconciliation on the account.
3. Set As at to the statement's last date (End of last month fills it in) and click Show. From is optional and only changes the line counts.
4. Read the strip: Books, Statement (the closing balance from the imported statement), Difference and Lines to match.
5. Read the Bank reconciliation statement: Balance as per books, less money received in the books not yet shown by the bank, add payments made in the books not yet through the bank, add or less statement lines not in the books, Balance the bank should show, Balance as per bank statement and Difference.
6. If the statement file had no balance column, type the Closing balance on the statement and click Use this balance.
7. When Mark reconciled says everything is matched or ignored and the statement agrees with the books, click Mark reconciled up to (date) and confirm.
8. Click Export CSV or Print to keep the report with the month's papers.

**What happens next:** The account is marked reconciled up to that date (shown under Reconciled up to and on the Bank reconciliation page). The lines and matches up to that date are locked.

**Tips**

- Entries in the books not yet shown by the bank lists cheques not yet presented and deposits in transit; they carry over to next month.
- Entries dated before the first imported statement that already went through the bank can be ticked there and marked as already cleared.
- A Difference that is not zero usually means a statement line is missing (import the full period) or the opening balance was never entered.
- Only the latest reconciliation can be undone (Undo under Reconciled up to); that unlocks its lines again.

### Accounting: statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Draft (expense) | Saved but not recorded: no number, nothing in the books. Can be edited, recorded or deleted. |
| Pending approval (expense) | Above an approval limit; waiting for approvers. Not numbered or posted yet. Take back returns it to Draft. |
| Recorded | Numbered and posted to the books. Can only be voided. |
| Void | Cancelled with a reason; its journal entry was reversed. The record is kept. |
| Posted (journal) | The entry is in the books. |
| Reversed (journal) | A mirror entry cancelled it; both stay in the books. |
| Reversal (journal) | The mirror entry that cancels an earlier one. |
| Reconciled | A bank or cash line matched to a bank statement. |
| In use / Sold / Disposed / Written off (asset) | Asset status. Only In use assets are depreciated. |
| Not in the books / Posted / Already in books (asset) | Whether the asset's purchase has reached the ledger. Depreciation is charged only when it has. |
| Posted / Due / Upcoming (depreciation month) | On the asset schedule: already charged, ready to charge, or in the future. |
| Draft / Active / Closed (budget) | Draft can be edited or deleted; Active drives expense warnings (one per financial year); Closed is frozen until reopened. |

### Accounting: routines

**Every day**

- Record cash and bank expenses and other income the same day, with the bill or receipt number.
- Check Pending approval expenses and chase approvers if needed.

**Every week**

- Look at the Accounting Dashboard: cash and bank, receivable, payable and recent entries.
- Finish or delete old draft expenses.

**Every month**

- Reconcile every bank account against the month-end statement.
- Run depreciation for the month on Fixed Assets > Depreciation run.
- Review Budget vs actual and the Profit and loss for the month.
- Post any provisions or adjustments as manual journal entries.

**Every year**

- Create and activate next year's budget before 1 April; close the old budget.
- Check the trial balance, balance sheet and asset register at 31 March and export them for the auditor.
- Ask the partner to lock the finished financial year in Company > Financial Year Settings.

### Accounting: common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot see Record expense or New journal entry. | You only hold accounting.view. Ask an admin to grant accounting.manage in Administration > Users and Access. |
| Saving says the financial year is locked. | The date falls in a locked financial year. Use a date in an open year or ask a partner to unlock the year. |
| The GST fields are missing on the expense form. | GST is not switched on for the company. A partner can switch it on in Company > Tax Configuration. |
| An expense was saved but has no number. | It is a draft or is waiting for approval. Record the draft, or wait for the approval; it is numbered when posted. |
| I need to change a recorded expense. | Recorded entries cannot be edited. Void it with a reason and record it again. |
| There is no Reverse button on a journal entry. | It was posted automatically by an invoice, expense, payroll or similar. Void or cancel that source document instead. |
| The Reconcile button stays grey. | Enter the statement closing balance, tick at least one line and keep ticking until Difference to statement is zero. |
| No depreciation is charged for an asset. | Its purchase is not in the books. Open the asset and click Record purchase (or mark it as already in the books). |
| I cannot change an asset's cost or dates. | Depreciation has been posted. Reverse the depreciation runs from the latest back, then edit. |
| Make active fails on a budget. | Another budget is already active for that year (close it first), or the budget has no lines. |

## GST & Tax

GST & Tax shows the company's GST, TDS and income tax position straight from the books, and keeps a record of every return filed and every challan paid. Use it to see what GST is payable each month, check the sales and purchase registers before filing, prepare return figures, post the input credit set-off, record tax payments, track TDS your customers deducted, and plan advance tax. The actual filing and payment happen on the government portals (GST portal, income-tax portal, TRACES); here you record what was filed and paid, with the acknowledgement.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing partner, Partner | Hold every permission: view everything, record returns, payments, TDS credits and the income-tax worksheet. |
| Finance manager | Runs the module by default (tax.view and tax.manage): prepares and records returns, records challans, posts the GST set-off, maintains the TDS receivable tracker and advance-tax estimate. |
| HR manager, Project manager, Employee | No access by default. An admin can grant tax.view (read only) or tax.manage in Administration > Users and Access. |

**Before you start**

- Open the GST & Tax tile from the module launcher. The left menu shows: Tax Dashboard, GST Summary, Sales Register, Purchase Register, Returns, GST Payments, TDS, Advance Tax & Income Tax, Tax Calendar.
- Only people with tax.manage see the buttons that create or change records (Record payment, New return record, Add TDS credit and so on).
- The figures come from the books, so issue invoices, record vendor bills and expenses, and post payroll before you prepare a return.
- GST returns appear only if the company is registered for GST (Company > Tax Configuration).
- Tax payments are posted from a bank account. If you leave Paid from empty, the default bank account is used, so set up Company > Bank Accounts first.

2. Read the Tax computation (built from the books' profit).
3. In Worksheet inputs, optionally enter Profit to use instead of the books, the Tax rate % (30 by default for an LLP), Surcharge % and Cess % (4 by default).
4. Add Add-backs (expenses not allowed) and Deductions with Add a line, then click Save worksheet.
5. In Advance tax schedule, enter Estimated tax for the year or click Use the computed figure, then Save estimate.

**What happens next:** Tiles show estimated income tax, advance tax paid, TDS credit and balance payable. The schedule shows the four instalments (15 June 15%, 15 September 45%, 15 December 75%, 15 March 100%) with required, paid, shortfall and status.

**Tips**

- Income and tax are rounded to the nearest ten rupees.

### Record an advance tax or self-assessment tax payment

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner
- **Where:** GST & Tax > Advance Tax & Income Tax > Record payment (`/tax/income-tax`)

1. Pay the challan on the income-tax portal.
2. On the Advance tax schedule, click Record payment on the instalment with a shortfall (the amount is filled in), or use GST Payments > Record payment and choose Advance tax or Income tax (self-assessment).
3. For advance tax choose the Instalment or leave From the date.
4. Enter the amounts by head, the CIN / challan reference and Paid from, then click Record payment.

**What happens next:** The payment posts to the books (advance tax to an advance tax asset account; interest and penalty to a tax expense account) and the instalment's paid figure updates.

### Track ITR-5 and the tax audit report

- **Who:** Finance manager or partner
- **Where:** GST & Tax > Advance Tax & Income Tax > ITR and tax audit (`/tax/income-tax`)

1. In ITR and tax audit, click New record if ITR-5 or the tax audit is not listed.
2. Open the record, Prepare from the books, file on the portal, then Mark as filed with the acknowledgement.
3. Attach the acknowledgement under Acknowledgement and working papers.

**What happens next:** ITR-5 is due 31 July (31 October when a tax audit applies) and the tax audit report 30 September after the year ends.

### Use the tax calendar

- **Who:** Anyone with tax.view
- **Where:** GST & Tax > Tax Calendar (`/tax/calendar`)

1. Open GST & Tax > Tax Calendar.
2. Choose Look ahead (30 days to 1 year).
3. Filter by kind: All, GST, TDS, Advance tax, Income tax.

**What happens next:** Tiles show what is Overdue, due in 7 days and due in 30 days. Items are grouped by month, including missed ones from the last 30 days.

### GST & Tax: statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Return record created; figures not prepared and not filed. |
| Prepared | Figures worked out from the books; not yet filed. |
| Filed | Marked as filed with the date (and acknowledgement). Locked until reopened. |
| Late | Past the due date and not filed. A filed return filed after the due date shows Filed late. |
| Recorded / Void (payment) | A tax payment posted to the books, or cancelled with its entry reversed. |
| Certificate awaited / Certificate received / Matched in 26AS | Stages of a TDS credit in the receivable tracker. |
| Eligible / No GSTIN / Ineligible | Input credit status of a purchase line. |
| Matches / Difference | Books vs filed comparison result. |
| Paid / Overdue / Due soon / Upcoming | Status of an advance tax instalment or calendar item. |

### GST & Tax: routines

**Every week**

- Look at the Tax Dashboard and Tax Calendar for anything due in the next 7 days.

**Every month**

- By the 11th: review the Sales Register for last month, export the CSVs, file GSTR-1 on the portal and mark it filed.
- Review the Purchase Register and mark ineligible credit with a reason.
- By the 20th: prepare GSTR-3B, post the input credit set-off, pay the cash balance on the portal, record the GST payment and mark GSTR-3B filed.
- Pay last month's TDS (challan 281) on the income-tax portal and record the challan.
- Check Books vs filed for differences.

**Every year**

- At the start of the year: Returns > Create the year's returns.
- Quarterly: file TDS 24Q and 26Q (31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May) and update the receivable tracker from Form 26AS.
- Before 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March: update the income-tax worksheet and pay advance tax.
- After the year: tax audit (30 September), ITR-5 (31 July, or 31 October with audit) and GSTR-9 (31 December).

### GST & Tax: common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No GST returns were created for the year. | The company is not marked as GST-registered. A partner can update Company > Tax Configuration, then run Create the year's returns again. |
| I cannot change the figures of a return. | It is filed and locked. Click Reopen, give the reason, correct the figures and mark it filed again. |
| Delete is missing on a return. | Filed returns, and returns with an input credit set-off still posted, cannot be deleted. Reopen it or undo the set-off first. |
| Post input credit set-off is greyed out or refused. | There is no input credit for that month, or the set-off is already posted. Undo it to redo it. |
| The register figures do not match what I expected. | The registers read the books. Check that all invoices are issued and all bills and expenses recorded for the period, then refresh. |
| A payment was recorded with the wrong amount. | Void it with a reason (this reverses the books) and record it again. |

### GST & Tax: connections to other modules

- Sales: issued invoices and credit and debit notes feed the Sales Register and GST output tax.
- Purchases: vendor bills feed the Purchase Register, input credit and TDS payable.
- Accounting: expenses and income with GST feed the registers; set-offs and tax payments post journal entries; the profit used for income tax comes from the books.
- People (HR): TDS on salary from payroll (section 192) appears in the payable ledger and 24Q.
- Company: GST registration, tax configuration and bank accounts are set there.

## Projects

Projects tracks every client and internal project from plan to completion: milestones, tasks, the team, hours logged, the cost budget and what the project has earned and cost. Revenue and cost come straight from the books (invoices, bills and expenses tagged to the project), so the project profit and loss is always current. Milestones with an amount to bill can be turned into a draft invoice in one click, and budget changes above an approval limit go through Approvals.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing partner, Partner | Hold every permission: create and change projects, budgets, milestones, tasks and time, and raise invoices from milestones. |
| Project manager | Runs projects by default (projects.view and projects.manage): creates projects, plans milestones and tasks, manages the team, logs time and proposes budget changes. Can see sales but not create invoices unless granted sales.manage. |
| Finance manager | Can view projects, profitability and financials (projects.view); creates invoices in Sales. |
| HR manager | Can view projects (projects.view). |
| Employee | No access to the Projects module by default. |

**Before you start**

- Open the Projects tile from the module launcher. The left menu shows: Projects Dashboard, All Projects, Tasks, Timesheets, Milestones, Project Profitability.
- Creating or changing anything needs projects.manage; without it the pages are read-only.
- A client project needs the customer to exist first in Sales > Customers.
- Add employees in People (HR) so you can pick the project manager, team members, assignees and people on timesheets from a list (names can also be typed).
- Creating an invoice from a milestone needs both projects.manage and sales.manage.

### Create a project

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > All Projects > New project (`/projects/projects?new=1`)

1. Open Projects > All Projects and click New project (or use + New > Project in the top bar).
2. Enter the Project name, for example ERP rollout for Acme Ltd, and choose the Project type (IT solution, Nano-technology / R&D, Consulting, Internal, Other).
3. Choose the Client (optional only for Internal projects), Status (Planned, Active or On hold) and Priority.
4. Choose the Project manager, Start date and Planned end date.
5. Choose Billing method: Fixed price or Time and material. For time and material, enter the Default hourly rate (INR), for example 1,500.
6. Enter Contract value (INR), for example 12,00,000, and Budget (INR), for example 8,00,000 (or leave it empty to use the sum of the category lines).
7. Under Budget by category, split the budget into Labour, Materials, Subcontract, Travel and Other.
8. Add Tags (comma separated) and a Description, then click Create project.

**What happens next:** The project is created with an automatic project code (shown in the message). It appears in All Projects with its status, health and progress.

**Tips**

- A new project can only start as Planned, Active or On hold.

### Find, filter and export projects

- **Who:** Anyone with projects.view
- **Where:** Projects > All Projects (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open Projects > All Projects.
2. Click a status chip: All, Open, Active, Planned, On hold, Completed, Cancelled.
3. Type in Search (name, code, client, manager or tag), choose Health if needed, and click Search.
4. Click Export CSV to download the list.

**What happens next:** The table shows each project's status, health, progress, dates, contract value, cost and profit. Click a project to open it.

### Edit a project or change its budget

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > All Projects > (project) > Edit (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project and click Edit (or Edit budget on the Budget vs Actual tab).
2. Change the details, the Budget (INR) or the category lines.
3. Click Save changes.

**What happens next:** Ordinary details are saved at once. A budget change that falls under an approval rule does not change the project yet: it is stored as a budget change waiting for approval, and the message says so. A change no rule covers is applied immediately.

**Tips**

- Only one budget change per project can wait for approval at a time; withdraw it first to propose another.

### Follow a budget change through approval

- **Who:** Project manager (proposer); approvers set in Approvals
- **Where:** Projects > (project) > Budget change waiting for approval (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project. A card Budget change waiting for approval shows the proposed change and its approval badge.
2. Approvers decide from the approvals bell or the Approvals module.
3. To cancel the proposal, click Withdraw.

**What happens next:** Approval applies the new budget to the project and records it as a revision. Rejection or withdrawal leaves the budget as it was.

**Tips**

- Budget changes waiting for approval are decided in the Approvals module, not on the project page.

### Change a project's status (complete, hold, cancel)

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > (project) > Change status (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project and click Change status.
2. Choose Planned, Active, On hold, Completed or Cancelled.
3. For Completed enter the Actual end date. For Cancelled a Reason is required; for others the note is optional.
4. Click Save.

**What happens next:** The status changes and is recorded in the Activity tab. A cancelled project shows a warning and no more time can be logged on it.

**Tips**

- The actual end date cannot be before the start date.

### Delete a project

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > (project) > Delete (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project and click Delete.
2. Confirm Delete.

**What happens next:** The project is removed. Only a project with no time, invoices, bookings or files can be deleted; otherwise the message tells you why and you should cancel it instead.

### Manage the project team

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > (project) > Team tab > Add member (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project and click the Team tab.
2. Click Add member.
3. Pick the Employee from the directory (or type the Name), enter the Role, Allocation (%) (for example 50) and Bill rate per hour (INR) (for example 2,000).
4. Click Save. Use Edit to change a member or Remove to take them off the team.

**What happens next:** The member is listed with hours logged. Removing a member keeps the time they already logged.

**Tips**

- A person can be on the team only once.

### Add and track milestones

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > Milestones > New milestone (or project > Milestones & Tasks > Add milestone) (`/projects/milestones`)

**Tips**

- The due date cannot be before the project starts, and the completion date cannot be in the future.
- Filter the Milestones page by status chip, search or project, and click Export CSV to download.

### Create an invoice from a milestone

- **Who:** User with projects.manage and sales.manage (for example a partner)
- **Where:** Projects > Milestones > Create invoice (`/projects/milestones`)

1. Find the milestone (it must have an Amount to bill and no invoice yet).
2. Click Create invoice.
3. The draft invoice opens in Sales. Check the lines, GST and dates, then save and issue it there.

**What happens next:** A draft invoice is created for the project's client with the milestone amount and linked to the milestone. Once the invoice is issued, it posts to the books and the project's revenue rises.

**Tips**

- The project must have a client.
- After an invoice is raised, the milestone amount cannot change and the milestone cannot be removed until that invoice is voided or deleted.

### Plan and move tasks on the board

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > Tasks (`/projects/tasks?new=1`)

1. Open Projects > Tasks and click Add task (or + New > Project task).

3. Click Save task.
4. On the Board view, drag a card to another column (To do, In progress, In review, Done) or use the arrow buttons on the card.
5. On the List view, change the status from the dropdown, or click Edit or Delete.

**What happens next:** The task gets an automatic task code. Moving it changes its status; the project's progress uses tasks done and milestones done.

**Tips**

- Filter by Project, Priority, Show and Assignee and click Filter. Click Export CSV to download.

### Log time on a project

- **Who:** Project manager or partner
- **Where:** Projects > Timesheets > Log time (`/projects/timesheets?new=1`)

1. Open Projects > Timesheets and click Log time (or Log time on a project page).

4. Choose the Person.

**Tips**

1. Open the project. The Overview tiles show progress, schedule, budget, revenue, cost, profit, receivable and hours.

**Tips**

### Keep project documents

- **Where:** Projects > (project) > Documents tab (`/projects/projects`)

1. Open the project and click the Documents tab.

### Projects: statuses

## People (HR)

People keeps the employee directory and everything about each person's work: department and designation, attendance, holidays, leave, salary, monthly payroll, performance reviews and project assignments. HR marks attendance and approves leave, payroll staff run the monthly payroll from attendance and leave and post it to the books, and every member uses the My pages to check in, apply for leave and download payslips. ## Partners

The Partners module keeps each partner's money position in the LLP: the capital account, the current account (drawings, remuneration, interest and profit share) and any loan the partner has given to the LLP. Every transaction you record here is posted to the books automatically, and large withdrawals wait for approval before they are posted. At year end you share the profit by the ratio in the LLP agreement and print each partner's capital account statement. The partners themselves (names, DPIN, designation) are added under Company > Partners and Signatories, not here.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Hold every permission: record transactions, approve or reject withdrawals (never their own), set the profit-sharing ratio, allocate and reverse profit, attach partner documents. |
| Finance Manager | Can view everything in the module (balances, transactions, loans, statements) but cannot record or change anything. |
| HR Manager, Project Manager, Employee | No access to this module by default. |

**Before you start**

- Add every partner under Company > Partners and Signatories. A partner who is not there does not appear in this module.
- Make sure the main bank account (and a cash account, if you pay in cash) exists in Accounting, because money transactions post to it by default.
- Know the profit-sharing ratio and any interest-on-capital rate from the LLP agreement.
- Withdrawals (Drawing or Capital withdrawn) above the approval limit wait for approval. The limit is ₹1,00,000 unless your administrator has changed it; setting it to zero switches approval off.
- Partner withdrawals are approved through the Approvals module. The example rule 'Partner withdrawals' asks every other partner to agree.

### Link a partner to their sign-in user and record the agreed capital

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Partners > click the partner's name > Overview tab (`/partners/accounts`)

1. Open the Partners module from the launcher and click Partners in the left menu.
2. Use Search (name or designation) and the Show box (Current partners, Former partners, All) to find the partner, then click the name.
3. On the Overview tab, find the Account settings card.
4. In Agreed capital contribution, type what the LLP agreement says, for example 5,00,000.
5. In Sign-in user, choose the partner's user name so the system knows this account belongs to them.
6. Add a short note if needed and click Save.

**What happens next:** The Capital account tile shows the agreed amount under it. Once linked, the partner sees 'this is you' on their own page, and the system stops them from approving withdrawals from their own account.

**Tips**

- Agreed capital cannot be negative.
- Linking the sign-in user matters for approvals: a partner can never approve a withdrawal from their own account or one they requested.

### Record capital introduced by a partner

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Transactions > Record transaction (`/partners/transactions`)

1. Click Transactions in the left menu, then Record transaction (or use '+ New' > Partner transaction in the top bar).
2. Choose the Partner.
3. In Type, choose Capital introduced.
4. Enter the Date and the Amount, for example 2,00,000.
5. In Paid by, choose Bank transfer, Cheque, UPI, Cash or Other.
6. In Received in, leave Default (main bank account, or cash when paid by cash) or pick a specific bank or cash account.
7. Type the UTR or cheque number in Reference (cheque no., UTR ...) and a Narration if useful.
8. Click Record transaction.

**What happens next:** The transaction gets an automatic number and status Posted. The books are updated at once: bank or cash is debited and partners' capital is credited. The partner's Capital account balance goes up.

**Tips**

- The hint under Type explains the accounting for each type.
- To correct a mistake, void the transaction and record it again; posted amounts cannot be edited.

### Record a drawing or a capital withdrawal (with approval for large amounts)

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Transactions > Record transaction (`/partners/transactions`)

1. Click Record transaction.
2. Choose the Partner and, in Type, choose Drawing (money for personal use) or Capital withdrawn (capital taken back).
3. Enter the Date and Amount, for example 1,50,000.
4. Choose Paid by and, in Paid from, the bank or cash account (or leave Default).
5. Add the Reference and Narration.
6. If the amount is above the approval limit, a yellow note says it will wait for approval. Click Record transaction.

**What happens next:** Up to the limit: status Posted straight away; a drawing debits the partner's current account and credits bank or cash. Above the limit: status Waiting for approval, nothing is posted yet, and an approval request (type Partner withdrawal) is sent to the approvers in the Approvals module. When it is approved the transaction is posted automatically; when rejected it becomes Rejected and nothing is posted.

**Tips**

- Capital withdrawn cannot be more than the partner's capital on that date.
- The person who records a withdrawal cannot approve it, and nor can the partner whose account it is (only an administrator is exempt).

### Approve or reject a withdrawal that is waiting

- **Who:** Another partner, Managing Partner or Admin (not the requester, not the partner whose account it is)
- **Where:** Partners > Transactions > Waiting for approval chip (or Approvals > Pending My Approval) (`/partners/transactions?status=pending_approval`)

1. On the Partner Dashboard, a yellow note shows how many withdrawals are waiting; click Review. Or open Transactions and click the Waiting for approval chip.
2. Check the row: partner, type, amount and reference. Click the number to see the full details.
3. Click Approve to release it, or Reject to refuse it.
4. For Reject, type the reason in the box that opens and confirm.

**What happens next:** Approve: the transaction is posted to the books (status Posted) and any open approval request for it is closed. Reject: status Rejected, nothing is posted, the reason is saved. The decision and the person who made it are shown in the transaction details.

**Tips**

- You can also decide from Approvals > Pending My Approval; both routes do the same thing.
- If balances changed in the meantime (for example capital is now lower), the approval is refused with a message.

### Record partner remuneration

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Transactions > Record transaction (`/partners/transactions`)

1. Click Record transaction.
2. Choose the Partner and, in Type, choose Remuneration.
3. Enter the Date (for example the last day of the month) and Amount, for example 75,000.
4. Add a Narration such as 'Remuneration for November 2026'.
5. Click Record transaction.

**What happens next:** Posted at once: remuneration expense is debited and the partner's current account is credited. No bank account is involved; pay it out later as a Drawing.

**Tips**

- Remuneration and interest already booked are part of the profit and loss, so they are already taken off the profit you share at year end.

### Record interest on capital

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Transactions > Record transaction (`/partners/transactions`)

1. Click Record transaction, choose the Partner and, in Type, choose Interest on capital.
2. Enter Rate (% a year), for example 12.
3. Enter Interest period from and Interest period to, for example 01-04-2026 to 31-03-2027.

5. Check or adjust the Amount, set the Date and click Record transaction.

**What happens next:** Posted at once: interest expense is debited and the partner's current account is credited.

**Tips**

- The calculation is simple interest on the capital at the start of the period.
- The rate must be between 0 and 100.

### Record a loan from a partner and its repayment

**Tips**

### Move part of the current account into capital

### Set the profit-sharing ratio

4. Enter each active partner's share, for example 50, 30 and 20. The total line must read 100.00% (adds up).

### Allocate the year's profit to the partners

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Partners > Profit Sharing > Allocations tab > Allocate profit (`/partners/profit-sharing`)

1. Finish the year's bookkeeping first so the profit and loss is final.
2. Open Profit Sharing; the Allocations tab opens first. Click Allocate profit.
3. Check Period from and Period to (they default to the current financial year up to today), for example 01-04-2026 to 31-03-2027.
4. If something must be set aside before sharing, enter Other deductions (for example 50,000 for a reserve) and a Note on deductions.
5. Click Preview. Read any warnings, check Net profit from the books, Profit to share and each partner's amount.
6. Click Save as draft.
7. Click the period in the list to open the draft. If the books changed, click Refresh figures.
8. Optionally enter a Post on date (it defaults to the end of the period) and click Post to the books.

**What happens next:** Posting gives the allocation a number and status Posted. Each partner's current account is credited with their share (debited if it is a loss) against retained earnings. Amounts are rounded to the paisa; any odd paisa goes to the partner with the largest share.

**Tips**

- The ratio in force at the end of the period is used for the whole period; a warning tells you if the ratio changed inside it.
- You cannot post a period that overlaps an allocation already posted; reverse that one first.
- Nothing can be posted when there is no profit or loss to share.

### Reverse a posted allocation or delete a draft

- **Where:** Partners > Profit Sharing > Allocations tab > click the period (`/partners/profit-sharing`)

**What happens next:** Reversing takes the credits back out of the partners' current accounts; the allocation stays in the list as Reversed with the reason shown. You can then allocate the period again.

2. Look for the yellow note about withdrawals waiting for approval and click Review if it appears.
3. Read the tiles: Total capital, Partners, Loans from partners, Drawings this year, Profit share allocated. Click a tile to open the matching page.
4. Check the charts: Profit-sharing ratio, Capital by partner, Drawings this year, Capital over time, Contribution against profit share.
5. If a red note says the partner records and the ledger do not agree, ask Accounts to look for manual journal entries to the partner accounts.

**What happens next:** You see the financial year's position at a glance; nothing is changed.

### Partners: statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Posted | The transaction is recorded and posted to the books. |
| Waiting for approval | A drawing or capital withdrawal above the approval limit; nothing is posted until it is approved. |
| Rejected | The withdrawal was refused with a reason; nothing was posted. |
| Void | Cancelled with a reason; if it had been posted, the journal entry was reversed. The record is kept. |
| Draft (allocation) | A profit allocation that is saved but not posted; it can be refreshed or deleted. |
| Posted (allocation) | The shares are credited to the partners' current accounts and the allocation has a number. |
| Reversed (allocation) | A posted allocation that was taken back out of the current accounts, with a reason. |
| In force (ratio) | The profit-sharing ratio that applies today. |

### Partners: routines

**Every day**

- Record capital, drawings and loan movements on the day the money moves, with the UTR or cheque number.

**Every week**

- Check the Partner Dashboard for withdrawals waiting for approval and remind approvers.

**Every month**

- Record each partner's remuneration.
- Compare partner balances with the bank statement and the Accounting reports; act on any 'records and ledger do not agree' note.

**Every year**

- Record interest on capital for the year using Work out interest on opening capital.
- After the books are closed, allocate the year's profit and post it.
- Print or export each partner's capital account statement for the financial year.
- Add a new profit-sharing ratio if the LLP agreement changed, and attach updated partner documents.

### Partners: common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Message: capital on that date is lower, more than that cannot be withdrawn. | Capital withdrawn cannot exceed the partner's capital on the transaction date. Check the date and amount, or record a Drawing from the current account instead. |
| Message: You cannot approve a withdrawal from your own account or one you requested. | Ask another partner, the Managing Partner or an administrator to approve it. |
| Message: Set the profit-sharing ratio (effective on or before the end of the period) first. | Go to Profit Sharing > Ratio > New ratio and add a ratio dated on or before the end of the period. |
| Message: A profit allocation covering part of this period is already posted. Reverse it first. | Open the posted allocation, click Reverse, then post the new one. |
| A ratio has no Remove button. | It has been used by a profit allocation. Add a newer ratio with a later Effective from date instead. |
| Voiding is refused because the capital or loan would go negative. | Void the later withdrawals or repayments first, then void the original transaction. |
| There is no Download PDF button on the statement. | The PDF statement is not switched on for this installation. Use Print or Export CSV. |
| A withdrawal above the limit was posted immediately without anyone approving it. | This happens when no approval rule matches and the person recording it holds the partners permission. Ask an administrator to keep the 'Partner withdrawals' rule switched on in Approvals > Approval Rules. |

### Partners: connections to other modules

- Company module: the partners themselves (Partners and Signatories) and the LLP agreement versions (Company Documents).
- Accounting: every posted transaction and profit allocation creates a journal entry against bank or cash, partners' capital, partners' current account, remuneration or interest expense, and retained earnings.
- Reports: net profit for the allocation comes from the profit and loss statement.
- Approvals: large withdrawals create a 'Partner withdrawal' request; approving it posts the transaction, rejecting it marks it Rejected.
- Compliance: partner changes recorded as legal events can add the LLP Form 4 filing to the compliance calendar.

## Approvals

The Approvals module is one inbox for everything that needs a second person's yes before it goes ahead: purchase orders, vendor bills, payments, expenses, leave, payroll runs, salary revisions, project budgets, partner withdrawals and quotations. Approval rules decide who must approve, in how many steps and for which amounts. When the last approver says yes, the record is released automatically in its own module (for example the bill is posted to the books). Nobody approves their own request, not even a partner who holds every permission.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Every signed-in user | Sees Pending My Approval (requests waiting for them) and My Requests (what they sent). Approves or rejects when they are an approver for the current step. Withdraws and resends their own requests. |
| Finance Manager, HR Manager, Project Manager | Also see All Approvals, Approval Analytics and Approval Rules (read only). The HR Manager can also reassign an approval step to another person. |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Hold every permission: see everything, create and change rules, withdraw or resend anyone's request, reassign steps. They still cannot approve their own requests (only an administrator, when nobody else at all could approve, is allowed to). |
| Employee | Pending My Approval and My Requests only. |

**Before you start**

- Each person's role must be set correctly in Access, because rule steps usually say 'anyone with a role' (for example Finance Manager).
- The first time a request is sent, the system creates editable example rules, for example: purchase orders above ₹50,000 (Finance Manager, then Managing Partner); vendor bills above ₹1,00,000 (Managing Partner); leave requests (HR Manager); expenses of ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 (Finance Manager) and above ₹50,000 (Finance Manager, then Managing Partner); payroll runs (Managing Partner); salary increases (HR Manager, then Managing Partner); project budgets (Managing Partner); payments above ₹50,000 (Managing Partner); partner withdrawals (every partner); quotations above ₹5,00,000 (Managing Partner).
- Requests are created from the other modules (for example when a bill or expense is saved above the rule amount, or with a 'Request approval' button). You do not create them in this module.

**Tips**

- Click 'Open record' under a title to look at the purchase order, bill or other record before deciding.

### Approve a request

1. Find the request in Pending my approval, or click its reference to open the full request.
2. Check the Details card (type, amount, requested by, rule applied) and the Approval steps.
3. Click Approve.
4. Type a Comment (optional), for example 'Rates checked against last order'.
5. Click Approve in the dialog.

### Reject a request with a reason

2. Type the Reason for rejecting, for example 'Quote from a second vendor needed'. The reason is required.
3. Click Reject.

### Approve several requests at once

- **Where:** Approvals > Pending My Approval > tick boxes > Approve selected (`/approvals`)

1. In Pending my approval, tick the box on each row you want to approve, or the box in the header to select all you can approve.
2. Click Approve selected (n) at the top.
3. Check the list in the dialog, add an optional comment and click Approve.

**What happens next:** Each request is approved in turn. If some cannot be approved, the dialog lists them with the reason under 'Not approved' and the others go through.

**Tips**

- Bulk rejection is not available; reject one at a time so each gets its own reason.

### Open a request and read its history

- **Who:** Approvers, the requester, and anyone with the approvals view permission
- **Where:** Approvals > any list > click the reference

1. Click a request's reference in any list.
2. Details shows the type, amount, requester, submission time, who it is waiting for (or when it was decided), age, the rule applied and a link to Open the record.
3. Approval steps shows each step, who can approve it, its due time, and each decision with comment. 'everyone must approve' marks an all-approvers step; 'reassigned' marks a step handed to someone else.

**What happens next:** Nothing changes; you get the full trail. The history cannot be edited or deleted.

**Tips**

- 'Sent again after ...' shows that the request is a resubmission of an earlier one.

### Track your own requests

**What happens next:** The tab shows how many of your requests are open, for example 'My requests (2 open)'.

### Withdraw a request

### Reassign an approval step to someone else (delegation)

- **Where:** Approvals > open the request > Reassign

1. Open the request (it must be Pending or Escalated).
2. Click Reassign.

4. In New approver, choose the person. The requester is not offered.
5. Add a Note (optional), for example 'Finance Manager on leave this week', and click Reassign.

**What happens next:** Only the chosen person can approve that step now. The step shows 'reassigned' and the history records who reassigned it to whom.

**Tips**

- There is no automatic out-of-office delegation; reassign each request as needed.

### Create an approval rule (amount bands, several steps, any or all)

- **Who:** Admin, Managing Partner or Partner
- **Where:** Approvals > Approval Rules > New rule (`/approvals/rules`)

1. Click Approval Rules, then New rule.
2. Enter a Rule name, for example 'Vendor bills 1 to 5 lakh', and choose what it Applies to (for example Vendor bill).

**Tips**

Partners and administrators hold every permission, so keep rules in place for things you want checked.

### Know what approving does for each type

- **Where:** Approvals > open the request > Open the record

6. Payroll run: the run moves to Approved.

8. Project budget: the budget change is approved.
9. Partner withdrawal: the drawing or capital withdrawal is posted to the books.
10. Quotation: the quotation is issued (numbered, with its PDF).

**Tips**

## Compliance

The Compliance module keeps the LLP on the right side of the law. It holds a ready-made catalogue of statutory obligations for an Indian LLP (MCA/ROC, income tax, GST, TDS, labour and local), turns it into a dated calendar for each financial year, and records each filing with its acknowledgement number, fee and any late fee. It also keeps the LLP's legal history, contracts and renewals, legal notices and court cases, company policies, and one Expiry Watch list of everything coming due.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Hold every permission: generate the calendar, mark filings, edit obligations, and manage legal events, contracts, cases and policies. |
| Finance Manager | Can view and manage compliance by default, the same as the partners. |
| HR Manager, Project Manager, Employee | No access to this module by default. |

**Before you start**

- Fill in the company profile (LLPIN, PAN, TAN, GSTIN, registered office, date of incorporation) and the partners under Company. The module reads these and never copies them.
- Open Obligations once and switch off anything that does not apply (for example GST if you are not registered), and switch on what does (for example the tax-audit version of ITR-5).
- Make sure the people who file returns have user accounts, so filings can be assigned to them.

### Review the obligations catalogue and switch items on or off

- **Who:** Admin, Partner or Finance Manager
- **Where:** Compliance > Obligations (`/compliance/obligations`)

1. Click Obligations in the left menu. Obligations are grouped by category (MCA / ROC, Income tax, GST, TDS, Labour, Local, Other).
2. Use Category, Switched (On or Off) and Search (name, form, code or authority) to find items, then click Search.
3. Read each row: form, frequency, when it is due, owner role and penalty.
4. Untick the box in the On column to switch an obligation off, or tick it to switch it on.

**What happens next:** The message reminds you to regenerate the calendar. Switching off does not delete items already in a calendar; regenerating the calendar applies the change to open items.

**Tips**

- Obligations you added yourself show 'added by you'.

3. Update Due date in words, for example '20th of the following month'.

### Compliance: statuses

**Every month**

## Workplace

Workplace is the office's day-to-day hub. Here you schedule meetings with agendas, record decisions and action items, and issue numbered minutes. Everyone keeps their tasks on a board, management publishes announcements and formal circulars (with acknowledgement tracking), and the office keeps an asset register and a visitor log.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Hold every permission: schedule meetings, issue minutes, assign tasks to anyone, publish announcements, issue circulars, keep the asset register and visitor log. |
| HR Manager, Project Manager | Have the workplace manage permission by default, so they can do the same as the partners in this module. |
| Finance Manager, Employee | View meetings and circulars, acknowledge circulars, read announcements, and create and work on their own tasks. They cannot schedule meetings, assign tasks to others, or open Assets and Visitors. |

**Before you start**

- Every colleague who should attend meetings, get tasks or receive circulars needs a user account with the right role (Access module).
- Circulars and minutes are printed on the company letterhead from the Company module, so the company profile should be complete.
- Announcements and circulars can be sent to everyone or only to selected roles.

### Start your day on Workplace Home

- **Who:** Everyone
- **Where:** Workplace > Workplace Home (`/workplace`)

1. Open the Workplace module from the launcher; Workplace Home opens first.
2. Read the tiles: Meetings this week, My open tasks, Overdue, To acknowledge. Click a tile to open the list behind it.
3. Check Upcoming meetings, My tasks due, Overdue tasks and Announcements.
4. If 'Circulars waiting for your acknowledgement' appears, click Read and acknowledge.

**What happens next:** Nothing changes; you see what needs your attention. New task and Schedule meeting buttons are at the top.

### Schedule a meeting with attendees and an agenda

- **Who:** Admin, Partners, HR Manager, Project Manager
- **Where:** Workplace > Meetings > Schedule meeting (or '+ New' > Meeting) (`/workplace/meetings`)

1. Click Meetings, then Schedule meeting. (In Month view you can also click a date number.)
2. Enter the Title, for example 'Monthly partners review', and choose the Type (Partners, Board, Team, Client or Vendor).
3. Enter the Date, Start time and End time, the Venue and/or the Online meeting link (must start with https://).
4. Choose the Organiser (leave as Me for yourself).
5. If it repeats, choose Repeats (Every week or Every month) and Further meetings to create, for example 3.
6. Under Attendees from the team, tick the colleagues. Click Add outside attendee for guests and enter their name and designation or company.
7. Under Agenda, click Add agenda item and enter each Item, its Owner and Minutes, for example 'Cash flow review', 'Ravi', 20.
8. Click Schedule meeting.

**What happens next:** The meeting gets a meeting ID (shown in the message) and opens on its own page with status Scheduled. For a repeating meeting the further meetings are created at once, one per week or month.

**Tips**

- End time must be after start time.

### Edit a meeting or create the next meetings in a series

- **Who:** Admin, Partners, HR Manager, Project Manager
- **Where:** Workplace > Meetings > open the meeting > Edit / Create next meetings (`/workplace/meetings`)

1. Open the meeting from the calendar or the All meetings tab.
2. Click Edit to change details, attendees or agenda, then click Save meeting.
3. For a repeating meeting, click Create next meetings, enter How many more (1 to 52) and click Create meetings.

**What happens next:** New meetings follow the latest one in the series with the same attendees and agenda; dates that already exist are skipped.

### Reschedule, cancel, reopen, mark as held or delete a meeting

- **Who:** Admin, Partners, HR Manager, Project Manager
- **Where:** Workplace > Meetings > open the meeting (`/workplace/meetings`)

1. To move it: click Reschedule, enter the New date, times and a Reason, and click Reschedule.
2. To call it off: click Cancel meeting, enter the reason (required) and confirm.
3. To bring back a cancelled meeting: click Reopen.
4. After it takes place: click Mark as held.
5. To remove it completely: click Delete and confirm. Delete is shown only while no minutes have been issued.

**What happens next:** The status changes to Rescheduled, Cancelled, Scheduled or Held, and the reason is shown on the meeting. Deleting removes the meeting, agenda and attendee list; action items already created stay in Tasks.

1. In the Decisions card, type the decision in Add a decision, for example 'Approve purchase of two laptops up to ₹1,20,000'.

3. To remove a decision, click the × next to it.

**What happens next:** The decision is listed on the meeting and is pre-filled into the minutes.

### Add action items from a meeting

1. In the Action items card, click Add action item.

### Record and issue the minutes of meeting

- **Who:** Admin, Partners, HR Manager, Project Manager

1. Open the meeting and click Record minutes. The form is pre-filled from the meeting, agenda, decisions and action items.

### See only your tasks (My tasks)

## Reports

The Reports module gathers figures from Sales, Purchases, Accounting, Payroll, Projects, Partners and Tax into ready-made reports and an executive dashboard. Reports only read data: nothing you do here changes an invoice, bill or entry. Every report can be filtered by period and downloaded as a CSV file that opens in a spreadsheet. Each person sees only the reports their role allows.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | See every report and every dashboard tile. |
| Finance Manager | Sees Annual Revenue, Project Profitability, Partner Capital, Salary Report, Pending Customer Payments, Vendor Outstanding, GST Summary and Cash-Flow Analysis (by default). |
| HR Manager | Sees the Salary Report and the Headcount tile on the dashboard (by default); also Project Profitability because the role can view projects. |
| Project Manager | Sees Project Profitability, Pending Customer Payments and Vendor Outstanding, plus the Headcount and Open CRM deals tiles (by default). |
| Employee | Has no access to Reports by default. |

**Before you start**

- You need the permission "Open the reports and analytics pages" (reports.view). Each report also needs the view permission of the module it reads, for example Salary Report needs "See salaries; prepare and run payroll". An administrator can change this in Administration > Access.
- Reports are only as good as the records behind them: issue invoices, record bills and payments, and post payroll before you rely on the figures.
- The financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. A year is written like 2026-27.

### Open the Reports home page

- **Who:** Anyone with reports.view
- **Where:** Reports > Reports Home (`/reports`)

1. On the module launcher, click the Reports tile.
2. In the left menu, click Reports Home.
3. Read the cards: each card is one report you are allowed to open and shows a live headline figure, for example "Owed by customers ₹4,25,000.00".
4. Click a card to open that report, or click "Open the dashboard" on the Executive dashboard card.

**What happens next:** Only the reports your role may see are listed. If a headline cannot be worked out it shows "Headline unavailable" and a dash; the report itself may still open.

**Tips**

- If the page says "Your role does not include any of the reports yet.", ask an administrator to give your role the right permissions.
- Headlines use each report's default period: the current financial year, or all dates for the reports that allow it.

### Read the Executive dashboard

- **Who:** Anyone with reports.view
- **Where:** Reports > Executive Dashboard (`/reports/dashboard`)

1. Click Executive Dashboard in the left menu.
2. Read the tiles. Depending on your role you see Revenue, Expenses, Profit and Cash and bank (needs accounting view), Receivables (sales view), Payables (purchases view), Headcount (HR view) and Open CRM deals (CRM view).
3. Click a tile to go to the report or page behind it, for example Receivables opens Pending Customer Payments.
4. Scroll down to "Six-month trend" to see revenue and expenses for each of the last six months as a chart and a table with a Profit column.

**What happens next:** Revenue, Expenses and Profit cover the financial year to date. Receivables, Payables, Headcount and Cash and bank are as of today. Profit shows in red when negative.

**Tips**

- The dashboard has no period filter and no CSV export; use the individual reports for that.
- Headcount counts employees who are working or on notice.

### Choose the period of a report

- **Who:** Anyone who can open the report
- **Where:** Any report > Period

1. Open any report from the left menu.
2. In the Period box choose "Financial year 2026-27" (or one of the four years before it), or "Custom date range".
3. For a custom range, fill From and To, for example From 01-04-2026 To 30-09-2026.
4. On Project Profitability, Partner Capital, Pending Customer Payments and Vendor Outstanding you can also choose "All dates"; these reports start on All dates.

**What happens next:** The figures reload as soon as the period changes. The other reports start on the current financial year.

**Tips**

### Export a report to CSV

- **Who:** Anyone who can open the report
- **Where:** Any report > Export CSV

1. Open the report and choose the period you want.

## Documents

The Documents module is where you write and issue official letters, minutes of meetings and operations documents on the company letterhead, each with its own unique ID and ready-to-print PDFs. It also keeps the company document repository (certificates, agreements, proofs) with version history and access levels. Separately, many records across the system (employees, vendors, projects, bills and more) have their own attachments section for supporting files.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Every role (by default) | Can issue letters, minutes and operations documents (permission documents.issue) and download issued PDFs. |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Also void issued documents, add or change letterhead templates and operations, verify or reject repository documents, and store Restricted documents. |
| Finance Manager | Uploads documents to the repository, edits their details and uploads new versions; sees Confidential documents. |
| Other roles | See and download Internal repository documents only, unless an administrator grants more. |

**Before you start**

- Set up the letterhead first under Company > Branding and Letterhead, so issued PDFs carry the right logo, address and footer.
- An issued document can never be edited. Check spelling, dates and names before you click Issue.
- Letterhead templates and the operations list are created automatically the first time they are opened (LHT-001 to LHT-008 and OPS-001 to OPS-010).

### Write and issue a letter

2. In "New letter", choose the Letterhead template, for example "LHT-003 Employee & HR Letters", or "No template (standard letter numbering)".
3. Choose the Document type: Business letter, Offer letter, Appointment letter, Experience letter, Project handover letter or Project completion certificate.

## Administration

Administration is where you control who can sign in and what each person may do. You add and switch off users, set passwords, decide each role's permissions, and invite new members with a one-time link so they can create their own account. You also set up how the app's emails look (sender, reply-to, signature) and check every email it has sent. It also keeps the login history and a permanent record of every change made to users, permissions and company settings.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do here |
| --- | --- |
| Admin, Managing Partner, Partner | Hold every permission, always: manage users and the permission matrix, view login history and changes, view the change history, set up email and see every sent email (email.manage), and invite members with any access role (partners cannot give the Admin role). |
| HR Manager | Can invite new members (users.invite by default), but may only give the access roles Project Manager or Employee. |
| Finance Manager, Project Manager, Employee | No administration access by default. An administrator can grant it in the permission matrix. |

**Before you start**

- Agree which role each person should have. Roles: Admin, Managing Partner, Partner, Finance Manager, HR Manager, Project Manager, Employee.
- The access role decides what a person can open. A job title (for example Site Engineer) is separate and grants nothing.
- The app sends email (invitation links, invoices, quotations, purchase orders, payment reminders and payslips) once your IT person has set up the mail server. Until then each message is saved on the server but not delivered, and the app says so. See "Set up email" below.
- Most changes ask for a reason; write something clear, because it is kept permanently.

### Invite a new member: create the link

- **Who:** Anyone with users.invite (Admin, Managing Partner, Partner, HR Manager by default)
- **Where:** Administration > Invite members > Create invite link (`/company/invite`)

1. Open the Administration module and click Invite members.
2. Click Create invite link.
3. Choose the Access role. The list only shows roles you are allowed to give: an Admin can give any role; a Managing Partner or Partner any role except Admin; an HR Manager only Project Manager or Employee. Employee is pre-selected when allowed.
4. Set "Link valid for (days)": 1 to 30 days. The default is 7.
5. Optionally fill "Name of the person" (for example Priya Raman), "Email of the person" and a Note. The name and note help you recognise the link; the email address is where "Email this link" sends the invitation.
6. Click Create link.

**What happens next:** The "Invitation link" window opens with the full link, for example https://your-erp/join/... The link works once and expires after the days you chose. The creation is recorded in the user and permission changes.

**Tips**

- The person joining cannot change the access role you chose.
- Someone who holds users.invite through a custom grant (not one of the roles above) may invite Employees only.

### Invite a new member: copy or email the link

- **Who:** The person who created the link
- **Where:** Administration > Invite members > Invitation link window (`/company/invite`)

1. Read the yellow warning: this is the only time the full link is shown.
2. To email it from the app, click Email this link. A window opens with the message ready: To (the email you entered), Subject "Your invitation to join ...", and a message with the role, the expiry date, the link and your signature. Change anything you like and click Send email.
3. Read the result: "Email sent", "Saved, not delivered" (email is not set up on the server) or "The email was not sent" with the reason. Click Done.
4. Or click Copy link and paste it into a chat or message to the person. If copying fails, click in the link box, select it and press Ctrl+C. "Open in my email program" prepares the same message in your own mail program instead.
5. Click Done.

**What happens next:** The link appears in the list as Pending until it is used, expires or is revoked. An emailed invitation is listed under Administration > Sent emails, with the link itself hidden.

**Tips**

- The full link cannot be shown again later, because only a scrambled copy is stored. If it is lost, use New link.
- Because the link is not kept, a failed invitation email cannot be sent again from Sent emails. Use New link and email that.
- Send the link only to the intended person; anyone holding it can use it once.

### Track, filter, revoke or replace invitation links

- **Who:** Inviters (each sees links they created or links for roles they may give)
- **Where:** Administration > Invite members (`/company/invite`)

1. Use Show to filter: All links, Pending, Used, Expired or Revoked.
2. Read each row: Status, Invitee, Access role, Invited by (with time), Expires and Joined (who joined and when, or who revoked it).
3. To stop a pending link, click Revoke, then Revoke link. It stops working at once.
4. To send a fresh link (lost, expired or revoked), click New link. The old link is withdrawn if still open, and a new one is made with the same role, name, email, note and validity length. Copy or email it from the window that opens.

**What happens next:** Revoking and replacing are recorded in the user and permission changes.

**Tips**

- New link is not offered for a link that has already been used.

### What the invited person does on the join page

- **Who:** The new member (no sign-in needed)
- **Where:** The link they received (/join/...)

1. Open the link. The page shows "Join" with the company name and "You are joining as" with the access role.
2. Fill the required fields: Full name, Username (letters, digits and . @ + - _ only, no spaces), Password (at least 10 characters, not common or purely numeric), Confirm password, Mobile number, Personal email, and "Your role in the company" (job title, for example Site Engineer).
3. Optionally fill Department, Date of birth, Address, Emergency contact name and Emergency contact phone.
4. Use Show / Hide to check the password.
5. Click Create my account.
6. On "You are all set", click Go to sign in and sign in with the new username and password.

**What happens next:** A user account is created with the access role chosen by the inviter, plus a member profile with the details entered. For Employee, Project Manager, HR Manager and Finance Manager roles an HR employee record is also created (joining date today, full time, with the job title and, if it matches an existing one, the department). Admin and partner roles get the account and profile only. The link is marked Used and cannot be used again.

**Tips**

- Full name and Personal email are pre-filled if the inviter entered them.
- If the page says "This link cannot be used", the link has expired, was already used or was withdrawn; ask for a new link.
- If it says "Please wait a moment", too many attempts were made; wait a minute and open the link again.
- "This username is already taken. Choose another." means someone already uses that username.

| Email settings says "Email is not set up on the server". | Your IT person must enter the mail server details (EMAIL_HOST, EMAIL_PORT, EMAIL_HOST_USER, EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD, DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL) in the server settings and restart the app. Then send a test email. |
| Emails fail with "The mail server refused the sign-in". | The mail server user name or password in the server settings is wrong. Ask your IT person to correct it, then use Send again on the failed rows. |
| I lost the invitation link. | It cannot be shown again. Click New link on that row; the old link is withdrawn and a fresh one is shown. |
| The role I want is not in the Access role list. | You may only give certain roles (HR Manager: Project Manager or Employee; partners: anything except Admin). Ask an administrator or partner to create the link. |
| "This link cannot be used" on the join page. | The link expired, was used or was revoked. Ask the inviter for a new link. |
| "This is the only administrator..." | Make another user an Admin first, then change or switch off this one. |
| Users and Access says "Your role does not include user management or login history." | Ask an administrator or partner for the users.manage or users.view_activity permission. |
| A user forgot their password. | Use Set password and tell them the new password in person. |

### Administration: connections to other modules

- Every module checks the permissions set here before showing menus, pages and buttons.
- Joining through an invitation creates an HR employee record for Employee, Project Manager, HR Manager and Finance Manager roles.
- Roles holding a permission are used to find second approvers (for example for bank detail verification).
- Change History collects changes from Company, Documents, Reports (salary exports), attachments, users and permissions.
- Email settings (sender name, reply-to, signature) are used by every email the app sends: invoices and credit notes (Sales), quotations and letters (Documents), purchase orders (Purchases), payslips (People), payment reminders and invitation links. Sent emails lists them all.
